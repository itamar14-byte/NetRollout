When finished: new rollouts are paused and running ones complete first. Everyone sees a notice until it's back.

Now: running rollouts are cancelled — devices already being configured get up to a minute to finish, the rest are left unchanged.

Queued rollouts that haven't started are cancelled either way, and everyone is signed out (sessions don't survive a restart).
