Restart Server

When finished: new rollouts are paused and running ones complete first. Everyone sees a notice until it's back.

Now: running rollouts are cancelled — devices already being configured get up to a minute to finish, the rest are left unchanged.

Queued rollouts that haven't started are cancelled either way, and everyone is signed out (sessions don't survive a restart). Cancel a rollout above to stop it alone - devices it hasn't reached are skipped.

Restarting when these finish.

New rollouts are paused; queued ones were cancelled. Cancel a rollout to restart sooner. This page reconnects when NetRollout is back.
