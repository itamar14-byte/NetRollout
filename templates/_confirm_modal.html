{# NetRollout's own question and message windows instead of the browser's confirm() / alert() (which a browser lets people switch off for a page - then confirm() answers No without asking): nrConfirm({title, message, confirmLabel, danger, badge}) → Promise, true only for the confirm button; Cancel, ×, Escape and a click outside answer false. A risky question (danger) is red and starts on Cancel. nrAlert(message, title) → Promise, closed by OK. message is text (never HTML).
